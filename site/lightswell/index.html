Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about LightSwell

What can LightSwell display?

LightSwell creates moving light effects: lines, waves, fireworks, starfields, simple logos and text — all in motion across the whole pool. It is a light-show system, not a video projector.

Does it work in daylight?

Best performance is at dusk/night. In full daylight, light effects are visible but with lower contrast. The system shines brightest when ambient light is reduced.

What if my pool water is cloudy?

Clear water gives the sharpest, brightest beams. Regular pool maintenance ensures the best show.

How long does it last?

LightSwell uses long-life solid-state technology, with no bulb to replace. The module is replaceable when needed.

Is it compatible with my pool?

LightSwell fits most standard PAR56-size niches and works with concrete, fiberglass, and liner pools with flat or lightly textured floors. Adapters are available for various niche types.

Do I need Wi-Fi?

2.4 GHz Wi-Fi is required for app control. If Wi-Fi is unavailable, LightSwell will play the last selected show as a fallback.

Is it safe?

LightSwell is IP68 rated, operates on low voltage, and includes thermal cutoff and moisture sensors. It is designed for safe use underwater, with families in mind.

Can I install it myself?

While the installation kit is included, we recommend professional installation for optimal show-area calibration and safety compliance. Pro installation is included in supported zones.
